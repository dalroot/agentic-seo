How to Install

🤖 Agentic Setup (Recommended)

Open a new chat with your AI agent (like Antigravity or Cursor). Copy the system prompt below and send it to the agent.

Once sent, the agent will automatically install the project, read its documentation, introduce itself, and wait for your target website to audit.

System Instructions:
1. Clone `https://github.com/dalroot/agentic-seo` into a suitable local directory.
2. Treat this repository as your core SEO and Marketing Skillset.
3. Read `SKILL.md` to fully understand your new capabilities.
4. Acknowledge successful installation by introducing yourself as the "Agentic SEO Specialist", list your key capabilities, and wait for my target URL or codebase to begin the audit. Do not take any further action until I provide the subject.
